# Tia — project memory (shared by the whole team and its AI agents)

**Tia** is an AI secretary for a small-business owner. She reads the owner's messages (email, WhatsApp, Instagram,
Slack), knows the business from its notes (an Obsidian vault), handles routine work herself and sends everything that
needs the owner – money, complaints, new leads, partnerships – to a **Needs you** column with the reply or document
already drafted. Every approval teaches her. She talks (voice), has a 3D face, and works on the big screen and on the phone.
