# Pitch video – recording script (3–4 min)

Rinalds records the screen and his voice; Tia's desktop app runs **full screen (F11)** on his PC with his own accounts.
Everything slow (lead search, AI) is sped up in the edit.

## Before recording (5 min)
- Start **Tia** from the desktop icon. Press **F11**.
- One-time sign-ins inside Tia (they stay logged in): **Instagram** (log in), **WhatsApp** (phone → Linked devices → scan the QR), **Slack** (your workspace), **Calendar** (Sign in with Google).
- In WhatsApp / Slack: open a chat → **Let Tia write** → **Put it in WhatsApp/Slack** → you press Enter.
- **Outlook** (classic) open in the background and signed in – Tia uses the RTU mailbox (Rinalds-Henrijs.Derics@edu.rtu.lv), not su.external.
- Check **Settings → AI** shows a working AI (Test → "Works").
- Delete old test drafts if you want a clean Drafts folder.

## How to talk to Tia on any page
- **Click Tia's picture** (bottom left) → talk → **click again** to send.
- Or type in **Ask Tia…** under her picture and press Enter.

## The script (menu order = story order)

| # | Page | You say / do | What Tia does on screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Tia** | "This is Tia, our personal AI secretary – all the apps a business uses, in one desktop app, and Tia can work in every one of them." | Her face, the desk |
| 2 | **Vault** | Talk: clients, decisions, projects, knowledge – all linked, so Tia understands the business and finds gaps. | Graph view |
| 3 | **Leads** | 🎤 **"Find me new leads and add them to the CRM."** | Searches the map, reads each website, scores the fit, rows fill in (~1.5 min → speed up) |
| 3b | Leads | Point at **⬇ Excel** / **⬆ Google Sheets** and the **✉ Email** buttons per row | – |
| 4 | **Mail** | 🎤 **"Now write up a Gmail draft to the newest lead."** | Opens Mail → Gmail · Drafts with the email written (real Gmail draft) |
| 4b | Mail | Talk: Gmail + Outlook in one inbox, reply or "Let Tia write" on any mail | – |
| 5 | **WhatsApp** | Talk: your real WhatsApp. Optional 🎤 "Send Ralfs a WhatsApp that the pitch video is ready" → **Send** in the card | Opens the chat, card next to Tia; goes out only on your tap |
| 6 | **Ads** | 🎤 **"Score my ads and tell me how to improve them."** | Opens Meta Ads, scores the newest ad, shows Tia's better version. Talk: analytics, future: ad variations + video from images (Higgsfield) |
| 7 | **Slack** | Talk: team channels in one place; Tia writes the messages | – |
| 8 | **Docs** | 🎤 **"Write up a contract for the latest company we interacted with."** | Referral partnership agreement PDF for that lead, from the vault template |
| 8b | Docs | 🎤 **"Now email it, put it in an Outlook draft."** | Outlook opens, Tia types the email with the PDF attached – you press Send |
| 9 | **Calendar** | Talk: Tia sees your Google Calendar; "Fill in with Tia" from one sentence | – |
| 10 | **Instagram** | 🎤 **"Review my Instagram statistics."** | The real Instagram on the left, Tia's score + what works + fixes + next post on the right |
| 11 | **Settings** | Talk: choose the AI model (API keys for a real business, or subscriptions); apps connect through **Composio** (free) | – |

## Rules Tia keeps (say once)
Tia never sends, pays or signs by herself: emails stay drafts, WhatsApp goes only on your tap, contracts wait for your signature.
