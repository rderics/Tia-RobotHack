# Stand script (3 minutes) — draft, the team edits this

1. **(0:00) Hook** – "This is Tia, the secretary of Oakline Studio, a kitchen studio in Riga. 12 messages came in overnight."
2. **(0:20) Start the day** – Tia reads them out loud, cards fly in, she sorts: 5 handled, 7 need the owner.
3. **(0:50) Explain** – open one card: *why* (reason, notes she read, rule). Show the **fraud email** she caught.
4. **(1:20) Human in control** – approve Laura's reply → **Outlook opens with the draft typed in** (not sent).
5. **(1:50) Contract** – "Prepare Ieva's kitchen contract" by voice → PDF opens → **printer prints it**.
6. **(2:20) Phone** – visitor talks to Tia on the phone (Expo Go or Telegram via Hermes); answer appears on the big screen.
7. **(2:40) Learning + limit** – approve a second lead reply → "I'll do these myself now". Show one honest mistake/limit.
8. **(3:00) Close** – "Tia never sends, pays or signs without you."

Backup: Wi-Fi fails → saved run (replay) with pre-recorded voice; printer missing → "Microsoft Print to PDF".
