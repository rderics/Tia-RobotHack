# Goal and judging (PCH02)

**Challenge:** "Show and Explain AI for Business" (partner: RBS & AI Studio) – a 3–5 minute **interactive stand**.

What the stand must show:
- A visitor gives an input (talks to Tia, or picks a message) and sees the AI work **live**.
- **Human in control:** the AI proposes, the owner approves. Visible on screen.
- **At least one mistake / limit is shown honestly** (e.g. Tia is unsure and asks; the guard overrides the model).
- **Explain:** for each decision, *why* – the reason, which notes she read, which rule applied.
- Repeatable and resettable (Reset button, saved run for bad Wi-Fi).

Our pitch in one line: *"Tia is a secretary that knows your business, does the routine itself, and never sends, pays or
signs without you."*

Two audiences ([[Architecture]]):
- **Non-technical judges:** big screen – 3D secretary, voice, inbox sorted live, fraud email caught, contract printed.
- **Technical judges:** a real phone talks to the same brain (Expo app / Telegram via Hermes), reads the same vault,
  runs a real skill, saves a real draft/PDF, remembers the edit.
