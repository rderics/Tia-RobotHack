# REFERRAL PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT No. {contract_no}

**Riga, {date}**

**{business}**, represented by {owner} (the "Studio"), and **{partner}** (the "Partner") agree:

## 1. Purpose
The Partner recommends the Studio to its clients who need a kitchen, wardrobes or built-in furniture made to measure. The Studio recommends the Partner to its own clients where the Partner's work fits.

## 2. How a referral works
- The Partner sends the client's name and contact (with the client's consent) by email.
- The Studio contacts the client within 2 working days and offers a free measurement.
- Drawings are exchanged in DWG or PDF.

## 3. Money
Mutual referrals only – neither side pays a referral fee or commission. Each side invoices its own client for its own work.

## 4. Quality and confidentiality
Each side is responsible for its own work and keeps the other side's client data confidential (GDPR).

## 5. Term
The agreement is valid for 12 months from signing and continues unless one side ends it in writing with 30 days' notice.
