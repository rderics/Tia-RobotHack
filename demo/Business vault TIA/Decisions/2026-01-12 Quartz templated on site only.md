# Quartz templated on site only
**Decided by:** [[Mārtiņš Ozols]] · 12.01.2026

## Context
Two quartz tops cut from our drawings did not fit old walls (1,2 cm off).

## Decision
[[Kokmateriāli Baltic]] templates every quartz top on site after carcasses are fixed. Install date for the top = template + 8–10 working days.
