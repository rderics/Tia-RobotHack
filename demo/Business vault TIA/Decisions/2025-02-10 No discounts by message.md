# No discounts by message
**Decided by:** [[Mārtiņš Ozols]] · 10.02.2025

## Context
A client asked for 10 % off by email after a delay; Elīna agreed by reply. It set a precedent two other clients quoted.

## Decision
Discounts, refunds and compensation are decided only by [[Mārtiņš Ozols]], by phone, never by message. Tia and the team answer: "Mārtiņš will call you today."

## Considered instead
- Fixed compensation table (rejected – every delay is different)
- Allow PM to give up to 5 % (rejected)
