# Bank details are only confirmed by phone
**Decided by:** [[Mārtiņš Ozols]] · 14.05.2025

## Context
Fake email pretending to be [[Kokmateriāli Baltic]] (domain kokmateriali-baltic.co) asked to pay invoice 2025-118 to a new account – see [[Fraud — fake invoices and bank-detail changes]].

## Decision
We never change supplier bank details because of an email. Any change is confirmed by phone with the known contact (Ojārs for Kokmateriāli).

## Considered instead
- Two-person approval of every payment (too slow for daily orders)
